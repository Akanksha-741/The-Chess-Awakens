♟️ DvChess

A scroll-driven chess landing page with a fully playable chess game. 
Chess pieces travel across the page as you scroll, and the final section lets you play a real match against the computer or a friend.


Features

Landing page

->Scroll-driven animation: a king and a knight move, scale and rotate between sections as you scroll
->Tilted 3D chessboard in the hero that flattens on scroll (CSS perspective transforms)
->Four sections: The Chess Awakens, Master Every Move, The Battle Begins, Checkmate is Inevitable
->Responsive layout, keyboard focus styles, and reduced-motion support

Chess game

->Legal move validation for every piece
->Check, checkmate and stalemate detection
->Moves that would leave your own king in check are blocked
->Highlighted legal moves, captures, last move and king in check
->Pawn promotion (auto-queen)
->vs Computer mode: a 2-ply lookahead AI that evaluates material and avoids losing pieces
->2 Players mode on the same screen
->New Game button
